# My Favorite Movie: Titanic 🚢

![Titanic](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/1/18/Titanic_%281997_film%29_poster.png)

**Titanic** (1997) is a *romance and disaster film* about the doomed voyage of the RMS Titanic.

---

## Quick Facts

| Detail   | Info                          |
|----------|-------------------------------|
| Director | James Cameron                 |
| Released | 1997                          |
| Runtime  | 194 minutes                   |
| Genre    | Romance, Drama                |

## Main Cast

- Leonardo DiCaprio as **Jack Dawson**
- Kate Winslet as **Rose DeWitt Bukater**
- Billy Zane as **Cal Hockley**

## Why I Love It

1. The love story between Jack and Rose
2. The amazing soundtrack by James Horner
3. The detailed recreation of the ship

> One of the highest-grossing films of all time, and winner of 11 Academy Awards, including Best Picture.
